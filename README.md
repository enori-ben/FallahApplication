## 📱 Overview

**Fallah Application** is a comprehensive agricultural store management system for inventory management, sales tracking, debt management, customer relations, and business analytics.

## ✨ Features

- 🛍️ **Product Management** - Add, edit, delete products with stock tracking
- 🛒 **Shopping Cart** - Complete checkout with tax calculation
- 💰 **Sales Tracking** - Daily, weekly, monthly revenue reports
- 📊 **Debt Management** - Track customer credits and payments
- 👥 **Customer Management** - Complete customer profiles and history
- 📈 **Analytics** - Interactive dashboards and business insights
- 🌍 **Multi-language** - Arabic & English support
- 🎨 **Modern UI** - Material Design 3 with Dark/Light theme
- 🖨️ **PDF Invoices** - Generate and share invoices
- 💾 **Data Export** - Backup and restore functionality
